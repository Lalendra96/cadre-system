# Requirements gap update

The new requirements comparison confirmed that the existing v12 package already contained employee timeline data, increments, retirement projects, reminders, data-quality workflow, reconciliation, workforce forecasting, notification polling, role-scoped responsibility, and Employee 360 links.

This update adds the remaining high-value quality and planning improvements:

- duplicate Pay No. detection;
- future appointment and joining-date checks;
- inactive-unit assignment checks;
- professional-registration expiry completeness checks;
- acting assignments without an end date;
- an age-band report (`under 30`, `30–39`, `40–49`, `50–54`, `55–59`, `60+`);
- aggregated retirement exposure by position;
- role protection for the age analysis route and navigation item.
