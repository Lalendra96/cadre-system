# Subject Officer Meaningful Action Centre Update — 2026-09-23

## Purpose
Align the Subject Officer Dashboard with My Workspace so identical HR concepts show identical counts and labels.

## Changes
- Dashboard retirement events now come from active scoped Employee records and the Employee `retire_date` accessor, matching My Workspace.
- Retirement workflow records are shown separately as:
  - Retirement Cases Not Yet Opened
  - Retirement Cases In Progress
- Increment due window is aligned to 30 days, matching My Workspace.
- Overdue increment actions are shown separately.
- Dashboard widget renamed from **Upcoming Due Actions** to **My Action Centre** and widened for clarity.
- My Workspace retirement wording now distinguishes employees approaching retirement from retirement workflow cases.

## Governance
The dashboard remains decision-support only. Employee events are derived from the officer's governed HR scope. Workflow case counts describe administrative follow-up and do not replace approval or record-management workflows.
