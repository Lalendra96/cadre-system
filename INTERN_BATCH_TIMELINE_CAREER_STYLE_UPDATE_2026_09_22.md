# Intern Batch Timeline – Career Timeline Style Update

Date: 2026-09-22

## Scope

The existing Intern Medical Officer Allocation batch timeline was redesigned to follow the visual and information pattern used by the Career Events & Workforce Timeline while preserving the existing Carder Management application shell and intern-allocation governance rules.

## Timeline information

Each dated batch now displays:

- Batch name and recorded period
- Formally responsible Subject Officer
- Operational status
- Next or final milestone
- RHO placed / total and pending count
- RHO completion progress bar
- Month-by-month visual batch period
- Automatic Today marker

## Status colours

- Ongoing – green
- Upcoming – blue
- Open / incomplete dates – amber
- Ended / pending lifecycle close – orange
- Closed / historical – grey

## Summary indicators

- Ongoing batches
- Upcoming batches
- Interns in active batches
- RHO placements pending from closed batches

## Governance safeguards

- Timeline dates come only from recorded batch start/end dates.
- The timeline does not infer, predict or recommend allocation or RHO decisions.
- Closed batches remain visible for historical traceability and RHO follow-up.
- Closed batches remain excluded from current allocation analysis.
- Existing responsibility and role-based write restrictions are unchanged.
